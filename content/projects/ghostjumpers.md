> Ever wish you could play Donkey-Kong with 16 of your best friends, at once?
> Ever wish you could jump over ghosts instead of barrels?

GhostJumpers is a mini-game from RetroWar that grew to be a fun single player game.

It's free and open source and a good example of how to code your own game.  You can [modify the source code](https://github.com/electronstudio/ghostjumpers) and change
the levels using [Tiled](https://www.mapeditor.org/)
